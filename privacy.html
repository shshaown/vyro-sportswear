THE SMALL PRINT

Clear by design.

Concept, not commerce.

VYRO is an original fictional sportswear website designed as a NEXORGE portfolio project. It is not associated with Nike or Adidas. Products, prices, sizing and product specifications are illustrative.

Your browser. Your bag.

The demonstration bag is stored locally in this browser. No account, payment or checkout service is connected. Removing all bag items clears your selections. The contact form only displays a local confirmation.

Creative assets.

Original AI-generated campaign imagery and custom real-time 3D garment studies are used to communicate the design direction. Images are illustrative rather than photographs of products for sale. Three.js and Manrope license notices are bundled with the website.
